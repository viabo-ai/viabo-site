# viabo.ai — media plan for the next update

The mock ships with no photography or video: every image slot uses the abstract line
illustrations in `src/components/Art.tsx`. This is the shortlist of what to capture or
produce next, in priority order, and exactly where each item goes.

## Ground rules for every asset

- **Consent first.** The site names no clients. Use crops that don't identify a site
  unless the owner (and, for partner-led work, the partner) has agreed in writing.
- **Privacy.** Blur faces, number plates, screens and anything with a name on it.
- **Outcomes, not method.** Show the twin, the register and the result. Don't show
  processing software, capture settings or workflow screens (protects the know-how).
- **Formats.** Stills as WebP, ≤300 KB, 1600 px wide. Video as MP4/WebM, ≤4 MB,
  10–20 s, silent, looping, with a poster frame. Alt text for everything.

## Priority 2 — one image per sector page, plus the lifecycle

Replace the illustration beside "What this enables" on each sector page.

| Page | Suggested image |
|---|---|
| Commercial property & retail | Car-park level with light fittings and CCTV registered |
| Residential & mixed-use development | Basement car park or plant room at completion, assets tagged |
| Airports & critical infrastructure | Generic back-of-house or plant area (non-identifying) |
| Logistics & industrial | Racking aisle with a clearance/width measurement overlaid |
| Museums & cultural institutions | Gallery with a display case placed from CAD, and a measurement |
| Education & public estates | Corridor or foyer with accessibility features tagged |

**Lifecycle page:** one small still or short clip per stage where you have it:
construction (services before close-in), handover (common property), operations
(an asset card with documents attached), upgrade (side-by-side comparison of two dates),
expansion (new layout placed in the twin). Stages without media stay text-only.

## Priority 3 — How it works

| Step | Suggested image |
|---|---|
| Plan | A redacted scope/deliverables page |
| Capture | Photo of a capture technician on site with the scanner (PPE on, no faces needed) |
| Tag & verify | The twin with tagged assets and one asset card open |
| Manage & keep current | The register list / an export, or a before-and-after pair of captures |

## Priority 4 — when available

- **Partners page**: partner logos (only with written consent); a redacted co-branded
  deliverable example.
- **Advisory & Innovation page**: short clips only if they are real and representative —
  a robot route drawn on a capture, a two-date comparison slider, an object placed in
  the twin. Otherwise keep this page text-only.
- **Team**: a simple team photo or founder portrait once you want a people section.
